INDUSTRIAL PROJECT / DSDA · 2024

6-DOF Racing Car Motion Simulator

Stewart–Gough Parallel Platform · 150 kg Payload

AXISPRO is a six-degree-of-freedom motion simulator based on a Stewart–Gough parallel mechanism, developed for immersive racing and flight simulation. Its six servo-driven linear actuators reproduce surge, sway, heave, roll, pitch, and yaw to provide physical motion feedback synchronized with a virtual environment.

Designed for a 150 kg payload, the compact platform combines coordinated multi-axis motion with an integrated mechanical structure.

01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

Project Overview & Technical Details

The project involved developing a compact 6-DOF motion platform capable of reproducing vehicle motion for racing and flight simulation applications. Its Stewart–Gough architecture uses six independently actuated legs between a fixed base and a moving platform, enabling coordinated translation and rotation while introducing coupled kinematics, actuator loads, workspace constraints, and potential mechanical interference.

MATLAB-based kinematic and dynamic modeling, simulation, and geometric optimization informed the platform dimensions, reachable motions, collision clearances, and actuator requirements for the specified payload. The resulting system was detailed in SolidWorks with a focus on manufacturable sheet-metal structures, machined actuator components, tolerances, and assembly fit.

Manufacturing incorporated laser cutting, CNC press-brake bending and forming, precision machining, rod chrome plating, and oven-cured finishing, followed by mechanical assembly and fit verification.

02 / PERSONAL CONTRIBUTIONS

My Role & Contributions

I was responsible for the mathematical modeling, mechanical development, and manufacturing oversight of the six-degree-of-freedom motion platform, working from initial engineering requirements through detailed design and mechanical assembly.

Modeling & Optimization:

Developed the Stewart–Gough platform's kinematic and dynamic models in MATLAB, and simulated and optimized its geometry with attention to workspace, desired motion, and collision avoidance between components.

System & Actuator Sizing:

Determined the platform geometry and overall dimensions from payload, weight, and motion requirements. Sized and selected servo actuators and ball-screw-and-nut mechanisms for the required travel and mechanical loads.

Mechanical Design & Visualization:

Designed the mechanical system and detailed assembly in SolidWorks, applying sheet-metal design experience, tolerances, and design-for-manufacturing principles. Produced project renderings with KeyShot.

Manufacturing & Quality:

Monitored the quality and dimensional accuracy of laser cutting, CNC press-brake forming, CNC machining, chrome plating, and oven-cured finishing; checked manufactured parts against intended fit and tolerances.

Hands-on Assembly & Integration:

Actively helped assemble the simulator, installing and fitting components, checking alignment and clearances, and resolving fabrication and tolerance issues as the mechanical system came together.
