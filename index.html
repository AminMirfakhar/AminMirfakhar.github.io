01 / WHAT I DO

Research meets engineering.

I work at the intersection of robotics, automation, optimization, and manufacturing—connecting rigorous methods with systems that have to work outside the lab.

Robotics & control: Kinematics, dynamics, motion planning, and control for robotic systems and industrial platforms.

Industrial automationl: PLC-based systems, machine vision, hardware integration, and production-oriented automation.

Intelligent manufacturing: Multi-objective optimization and non-planar toolpath generation for additive manufacturing.
